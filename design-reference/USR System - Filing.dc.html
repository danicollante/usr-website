Design system · direction “Filing”

The reusable system

Everything below is the vocabulary the rest of the site is built from: one paper surface, one serif for statements, one sans for reading, one mono for labels and data. Rules and hairlines do the work that cards and shadows usually do.

06

FAQ

Hairline-separated rows, mono index, question Spectral 24, answer 16/1.65 in a 64ch measure. First row open by default; the indicator is a gold rule, rotated — no chevron icons.

Q1
Where is the Cestos Project located?

In Nimba County, Liberia, on the Cestos shear zone, west of the Ity gold mine.

Q2
Is the project drill-ready?

Closed row: plus. Open row: minus. Row hover tints the question to #035D53.

07

Figures & maps

Maps and survey surfaces are documents, not decoration: white mount, 20px padding, 1px hairline frame, mono caption numbered “Fig. n —”. Never full-bleed, never cropped past the legend.

liberia-field-team-sampling.webp
[ drop photography — no white mount, no frame, full column width ]
Photography: bleeds to the column edge, caption optional.
survey data / prospectivity surfaces → white mount + frame + numbered caption
photography → unframed, edge to edge, no mount
portraits → 150px circle, striped placeholder until supplied
08

Header & footer

One flat level of navigation, no dropdowns. Current page carries a 2px gold underline. Primary CTA pinned right on every page. Footer is the only inverted surface besides dark bands.
